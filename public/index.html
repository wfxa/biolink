05 questions

Ask the pharmacist.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Making a page, styling it and reading your stats are all free. Premium only adds heavier extras.

Any side effects?

Mild cases of people asking where you got your page. In rare cases, rearranging your sections at 3am.

How does the Discord part work?

Join the Lanyard server on Discord and add your user ID in your dashboard, or simply sign in with Discord. Your status, game and song then show on your page and update every few seconds.

Can I change my name later?

Yes, any time. Your old name rests for 24 hours before anyone else can take it, and you can keep spare names that point to your page.

What's my number?

Every patient gets a number in the order they joined. It's printed on your prescription card and it never changes.

Something on a page isn't okay.

Every page has a small report link at the bottom. The team reads every report.
